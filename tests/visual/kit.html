Installation sequence

.steps, set with the sample installation panels B to F (see sheet 11).

  1. 1Base

    50 mm lean concrete, then a 150 mm grade 30N concrete base with one layer of BRC-A8.

    Panel B, item 2
  2. 2Anchors

    Stainless steel anchor hooks in the base.

    Panel B, item 3
  3. 3Set the tank

    Lower the tank onto the base and secure it with stainless steel turnbuckles.

    Panel C, items 4 and 5
  4. 4Fill and backfill

    Fill the tank with water, then backfill around it with sand.

    Panel D, items 6 and 7
  5. 5Pipes

    Inlet from the last manhole and outlet to the outlet manhole, both at 1:100.

    Panel E, item 8
  6. 6Slab and access

    100 mm 1:2:4 concrete slab with one layer of BRC-A9. Extend the brick sump for the manhole cover.

    Panel E, items 9 and 10
  7. 7Finish

    Sand backfilling to ground level and the cover for the manhole.

    Panel F, item 11
