How the model is chosen

  1. Up to 18 PE: PE Bio-Filter septic tank.
  2. 19 to 30 PE: FRP Bio-Filter septic tank.
  3. Deep burial picks FRP at any PE up to 30, for its flexural strength: Lintang Tankworks' guide rule.
  4. A high water table suits both bio-filter series, so the model stays the same. Anchor and strap the tank to its base.
  5. 31 to 150 PE: LT sewage treatment, which needs mains power for its blowers. 31 to 39 PE takes the LT-40.
  6. Two models at the same PE: vertical for a limited footprint, horizontal for a shallower dig.
  7. Above 150 PE: a full sewage treatment plant. Talk to Lintang Tankworks' engineers.
