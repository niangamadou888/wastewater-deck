High-strength FRP for high water tables and deep burial

8 to 30 PE. The same upflow bio-filter as the PE series: no pump, no power, no moving parts. The fibreglass shell has high flexural strength. FRP takes over above 18 PE, and for deep burial at any PE by Lintang Tankworks' guide rule. On a high water table it is anchored and strapped, as drawn.
