No pump, no power, no moving parts

PE and FRP Bio-Filter, 6 to 30 PE. Sewage settles in the first zone, then rises through plastic bio-media and leaves by a lower outlet. The flow runs by gravity alone.

Four states of the water

  1. 1 Raw Enters from the last manhole by the higher pipe and turns down into the settling zone.
  2. 2 Settled Solids settle in the outer zone. The flow passes under the filter chamber.
  3. 3 Filtered Rises up through the plastic bio-media: upflow anaerobic filtration.
  4. 4 Discharged Leaves by the lower pipe to the public drain.
Power
None. No lift pump, no moving parts.
Desludging
Every 2 years, through the separate settling-zone opening. Refill with water after.
Tank
Leakproof. Resists rust and most chemicals in soil and sewage.
