Aerated treatment designed for Standard A effluent

40 to 150 PE. A pre-fabricated FRP tank in two chambers. A diaphragm blower pushes air through a diffuser to treat the flow.

  1. RawSewage enters the aerobic reactor tank (ART).
  2. SettledSolids settle in the lower zone of the ART.
  3. AeratedAir from the diffuser treats the rising flow.
  4. ClarifiedThe final settlement tank (FST) clarifies it.
  5. DischargedTreated effluent leaves the outlet.
