Standards and authorities

SIRIM QAS
Malaysia's product certification body. MS 2441-1:2012 covers PE and FRP septic tanks, MS 2441-2:2014 covers small sewage treatment systems.
SPAN
Septic tanks built to the Malaysian Sewerage Industry Guidelines Volume V and the MS 1228:1991 code of practice.
IWK
Malaysia's national sewerage operator; desludging every 2 years is typical.
EQA
Environmental Quality Act discharge limits; an LT system is designed to Standard A.
