Install in 7 steps, desludge every 2 years

PE, FRP and LT. Lintang Tankworks supplies the tank. Your contractor installs it in this order. If the installation manual is not followed, the warranty is void.

The tank with its manhole covers. For LT, also two air blowers (1 duty, 1 standby) and the air diffuser.

Excavation, base, anchoring, backfill, pipes, slab and manhole necks. For LT, also installs the blowers. Blower housing: by others.

Installation sequence

  1. 1 Excavate

    Dig the pit with 300–600 mm of working space all round the tank.

    Item 1 · panel A
  2. 2 Base and anchors

    50 mm lean concrete, then a 150 mm grade 30 N base with one layer of BRC-A9. Cast in four Ø12 mm stainless steel hooks.

    Items 2–3 · panel B
  3. 3 Set and strap

    Lower the tank onto the cured base. Tie the Ø20 mm SS304 strap over it and tighten the turnbuckle.

    Items 4–5 · panel C
  4. 4 Fill and backfill

    Fill with water to top water level. Backfill with sand (fine sand recommended), compacted in 150 mm layers up to the rim.

    Items 6–7 · panel D
  5. 5 Pipes

    Compact the backfill at the inlet and outlet. Lay both pipes at a 1:100 fall.

    Item 8 · panel E
  6. 6 Slab and necks

    Cast a 100 mm 1:2:4 concrete slab with one layer of BRC-A9 and the manhole openings. Build brick sump necks to platform level.

    Items 9–10 · panel E
  7. 7 Covers and final fill

    Backfill with sand to platform level and fit the manhole covers. Fill the tank with water to full capacity.

    Items 11–12 · panel F

Item and panel numbers are illustrative (docs/SOURCES.md). Schematic panels, not to scale; invert levels per site, TBC.

Care

  1. Desludge every 2 years, in line with IWK servicing.
  2. Refill the tank with water after every desludging.
  3. Check for unusual odours between services.
  4. Installed properly, the tank lasts 10+ years.

LT start-up

  1. Seed it: fill 1/3 of the capacity with Standard A crude sewage (imported sludge).
  2. Fill the other 2/3 with water to top water level.
  3. The blowers need mains power and a weatherproof housing, by others.
